Ravenroot now keeps a durable, tenant-scoped inventory of process instances and their traversals, so
work already accepted before a restart remains discoverable afterward through `GET
/v1/executions/inventory`, `GET /v1/executions/{id}/traversals`, and the new `ravenroot inventory` and
`ravenroot traversals` CLI commands. Each instance reports a recovery status — active, waiting,
interrupted, parked, or terminal — computed from its stored state rather than a second record that
could fall out of step with it. Terminal instances are retained for seven days by default before an
operator or scheduled maintenance removes them, and every listing reports how far back its answer is
complete so an operator can tell a missing instance from one that aged out of retention.
