The design editor gains two additional automatic arrangements, `Arrange — Hierarchical (new)` and
`Arrange — Flow (new)`, next to the existing ones in the Layout menu. Both place nodes on discrete
layers, reserve room for node labels, route edges in channels between layers, and carry backward
edges in dedicated tracks under the drawing instead of across it. The existing `Hierarchical`,
`Flow`, `Organic` and `Keep positions` arrangements are unchanged.
